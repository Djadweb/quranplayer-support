Frequently asked questions

Audio does not play, or stops on its own

Recitations stream from public Quran services, so a weak connection can interrupt playback. Check that you are online, then try another reciter. If you downloaded a surah, it plays offline from the Downloads tab. If audio still fails, restart the app and try once more.

How do offline downloads work?

Open a surah and tap the download icon. Downloads are stored on your device and keep working in airplane mode. Deleting the app also deletes them.

A reciter is missing

Reciters are added as licensing and audio quality allow. Email us the name and we will look into it.

Background playback and the Lock Screen player

Playback continues when you leave the app, and the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show what is playing. If the controls disappear, press play once more in the app.

Widgets and Live Activities

Long-press the Home Screen, choose Edit, then add a Quran Player widget. Live Activities appear on the Lock Screen while a recitation is playing. Both can be turned off in iOS Settings.

Changing the language

Settings → Language. The interface supports many languages and the choice is remembered on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — settings, bookmarks, favourites, downloads and listening statistics — stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Quran Player (by Original Goods Ltd) does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data on your device

Your settings, bookmarks, favourite reciters, downloaded recitations and listening statistics are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Network requests

To play recitations and show surah information, the app requests audio and text from public Quran services (such as api.quran.com and audio.qurancdn.com and reciter audio hosts). These services receive your IP address as part of normal internet requests, under their own policies. The app sends no other information about you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
