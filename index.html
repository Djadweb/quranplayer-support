Quran Player – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Quran Player (by Original Goods Ltd) does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data on your device

Your settings, bookmarks, favourite reciters, downloaded recitations and listening statistics are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Network requests

To play recitations and show surah information, the app requests audio and text from public Quran services (such as api.quran.com and audio.qurancdn.com and reciter audio hosts). These services receive your IP address as part of normal internet requests, under their own policies. The app sends no other information about you.

Donations

The app may offer a link to an external donation page, opened in your browser. Payments are handled by that page, not by the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
